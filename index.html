Optional Analytics

Analytics Choices from Version 1.0.81

Starting with version 1.0.81, Firebase Analytics collection is disabled until you accept the app's optional analytics request. Android uses the native Analytics SDK; it does not also initialize web Analytics.

You can refuse, change, or withdraw this permission in Settings → Analytics preferences. Refusal does not prevent gameplay. Withdrawal stops further Analytics collection; it does not automatically erase data already collected. Contact us to exercise deletion rights.

Analytics permission does not authorize use of Analytics data for advertising personalization. Advertising choices are handled separately by Google UMP. Security, authentication, purchases, and cloud saves have their own necessary data processing.

Analytics Facoltative

Scelte Analytics dalla Versione 1.0.81

Dalla versione 1.0.81, la raccolta Firebase Analytics è disattivata fino all'accettazione della richiesta Analytics facoltativa dell'app. Android usa il SDK Analytics nativo e non inizializza anche Analytics web.

Puoi rifiutare, modificare o revocare il consenso in Impostazioni → Preferenze Analytics. Il rifiuto non impedisce di giocare. La revoca interrompe la raccolta Analytics successiva e non cancella automaticamente i dati già raccolti. Contattaci per esercitare i diritti di cancellazione.

Il consenso Analytics non autorizza l'uso dei dati Analytics per la personalizzazione pubblicitaria. Le scelte pubblicitarie sono gestite separatamente da Google UMP. Sicurezza, autenticazione, acquisti e salvataggi cloud comportano trattamenti necessari distinti.

Rewarded Advertising

AdMob and Your Choices

You can choose to watch an ad to obtain 3 virtual SC or 100 XP, depending on the selected button. The app requests the reward only after the advertising SDK confirms it was earned. Reward receipts are saved on the device until credited; signed-in rewards are also recorded on our server.

Advertising privacy choices are handled through Google UMP when applicable. Available privacy options can be reopened from the app settings when required by UMP. These choices are separate from the app's analytics preference.
